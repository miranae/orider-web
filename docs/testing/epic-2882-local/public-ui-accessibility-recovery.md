## Already-open public maps

Public detail now revalidates through the current public DTO on window focus, visibility return, and every 30 seconds while visible. Existing geometry is removed before each request and whenever the document becomes hidden. Each refresh has a 10-second local deadline in addition to the callable timeout. Network failures preserve text/comment drafts but keep geometry absent; authorization or account failures clear the detail. Late responses after backgrounding, unmount, or account/activity change are ignored. A separately scoped map snapshot prevents delayed social aggregate responses from restoring older geometry.

This is bounded polling under an active browser, not instantaneous revocation or a hard wall-clock guarantee when the browser suspends timers. Downloaded images cannot be recalled. Recovery occurs on the next successful focus/visible timer revalidation. Regression cases cover the cadence, temporary failure, access denial, backgrounded late response, and stale social readback. Three focused suites passed 51 tests at this checkpoint.
